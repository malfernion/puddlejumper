Puddlejumper
a tiny-ocean submarine odyssey
W A S D / ←↑↓→ swim toward a direction
Space rocket boost (refills under water)
E dock / talk / interact   L lamp   M chart
Build speed under water, then leap. Leap hard enough and you'll leave the world.
Headphones recommended · gamepad supported
Becalmed

Swim: WASD / arrows point the way; the sub turns and paddles toward it. "Up" is always away from the core.

Leap: get up to speed heading for the surface. Boost with Space while airborne to fly higher, or aim and burn straight up to escape the world entirely.

Fuel only refills while you're submerged. In space you can nudge yourself with the arrow keys.

Lamp (L): anglers in the deep are drawn to light. Darkness is safety… and darkness.

Depth: every hull has a rating. Past it, the hull groans and leaks.

Chart (M): everything you've seen. Ports and beacons appear once found.
